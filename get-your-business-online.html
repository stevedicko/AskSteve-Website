The Starter Pack

What’s included?

Your business website

Design and deployment of up to five static pages to explain your services and help customers contact you. A static website is a straightforward information website, rather than a complex web application.

Typical pages include Home, About, Services, Gallery or Testimonials, and Contact. We agree the final page list before work begins.

Your own domain and hosting

One available standard .co.uk domain, registration and configuration, plus the first 12 months of website hosting.

Premium domains and other extensions need a separate quotation.

Business email

One mailbox using your domain, such as hello@yourbusiness.co.uk, including setup and the first 12 months of email hosting.

Storage and supported access methods are confirmed in the quotation.

You provide your wording and images. Two design revision rounds are included. We agree the page list, layout and contact methods before the build starts. No copywriting allowance or stock-photo licence is assumed.

Questions small businesses ask

Frequently asked questions

Can I use an existing domain?

Yes. I will review your existing domain and agree any transfer or configuration work before making changes. Any additional charges are confirmed in your quotation.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes. Starter Pack and webpage setup costs can be spread over 12 months when you take the support plan. The Starter Pack with support is £160/month for 12 months, £1,920 in total. Your quotation explains payment dates and any outstanding setup balance if support ends early.

Can I have an online shop or booking system?

The Starter Pack is for an information website. Online shops, booking systems, customer logins, databases and bespoke integrations need a separate scope and quotation.

What do I need to supply?

Your business details, logo, wording, images and any required policy text, with permission to use them. Two design revision rounds are included. We agree the page allowance, including any policy pages, before work starts.

Is moving my old email included?

No. Moving existing email, calendars or contacts is quoted separately. Mailbox storage, device setup and access methods are confirmed in your quotation. Office software licences are not included.

Is this the same as Managed IT?

No. This plan supports the website and business email supplied here. For support covering business computers, printers and networks, see the separate Managed IT plans.

What happens after the first year?

Your domain, hosting and original mailbox renew at £180/year or £15/month. Optional support is £948/year or £79/month. Each extra mailbox is £60/year or £5/month. Your quotation confirms the renewal arrangements.
